Tutorial & Help

Importing word lists from Google Sheets or Excel

In Google Sheets or Excel, put the pair in column A and the word in column B. Select the cells and copy them, then paste into the Backup box in Settings and press Import. You can also download the sheet as a CSV (File, Download, Comma Separated Values), open it in a text editor, and paste that.

pair,word
BX,Box
HR,Hair
LQ,Liquid

Tab-separated pastes straight from a sheet also work, and the header row is optional. Pairs use letters A to X. Importing adds to your existing words and overwrites any matching pairs.

Speffz grid spreadsheets work too. Put the first letters (A to X) down the first column and the second letters across the top row, with the word in each crossing cell. Row G and column N gives the pair GN. Labels like words or X / SH are cleaned up automatically (X / SH counts as X), and empty cells are skipped. Every imported word is converted to lowercase.

,A,B,C
A,aqua,abbey,acorn
B,bat,bubble,
C,cat,cabin,cucumber

JSON is accepted too:

{"words":{"BX":"Box","HR":"Hair"}}

Where words appear

Saved words automatically appear directly below the timer and pair as soon as you stop the timer. In batch mode, each pair’s word shows in order.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • [Space] Cycle/Stop: start, stop, then load the next pair
  • [Esc] or [R] Reset: cancel the run without saving a time
  • [Tab] Edit Word after stopping, then [Enter] to save
  • [D] Lookup: find or edit any pair’s word
  • [Y U I O P J H K L N M B], punctuation keys, or [8 9 0]: mark the active pair as failed and reveal its saved word without logging a time

Randomization modes

  • 3BLD IRL (True Random): every draw is random from your active pool, with no forced cycling. The same pair never appears twice in a row.
  • Full Pool Non-Repeating: shuffles the whole pool and deals every pair once before any repeats.
  • Weak-Pair Hybrid Split (60/40): about 40% of draws come from your weak pairs and 60% from the full pool.
  • Strict Weak-Pairs Drill Only: only weak pairs. With none flagged yet, it uses your slowest tested pairs, or untested pairs on a fresh start.
  • Missed-Word Review: shows only pairs marked failed with a right-side fail key. Two successful single-pair recalls in a row remove a pair from this list.
  • Spaced Repetition: introduces unreviewed pairs, then prioritises pairs that are due. Faster recalls are scheduled further into the future; a failed pair becomes due immediately.

Weak pairs and graduation

A single pair slower than the weak-pair threshold is flagged. In Hybrid or Drill mode, finishing a flagged pair under the threshold 3 times in a row graduates it: it is removed from the weak list and the stats count.

Heatmap that follows improvement

The heatmap uses a recent-weighted average rather than treating every old attempt equally. Each new single-pair time shifts the colour toward your current speed, so a pair can genuinely move from red to green as you improve. Your complete history remains available for the session and leaderboard, but it does not permanently hold the heatmap back.

Missed-word review

Use a right-side fail key while a pair is active to add it to your missed-word list. In Missed-Word Review mode, only those pairs are shown. Two successful single-pair recalls in a row remove a pair from the list; failing it again resets that progress. Failed attempts never add a time to your stats.

Spaced repetition

Spaced Repetition first introduces pairs you have not yet reviewed, then prioritises pairs that are due again. A fast recall is scheduled further into the future than a medium or slow recall. A failed pair becomes due immediately, so it returns to practice sooner without repeating on the very next draw.

Real Orozco corner memo

Real Orozco Corner Memo generates a legal 20-move 3×3 scramble locally, simulates its corners, and traces a standard Speffz target stream from your C (UFR) buffer with B (UBR) as helper. When a new cycle is needed, it prefers G, then H, then the remaining available stickers. The result is split into your usual visual letter pairs and timed as one full corner memo attempt.

If the corner target stream has an odd number of letters, the trainer marks corner parity. It preserves the final single target instead of inventing a fake pair, matching your plan to pseudo-swap during edge memo so B/C are swapped before you execute edges, corners, and your final J-perm. This is a memo trainer: it creates the trace and parity cue, but does not check execution.

Lookup and leaderboard

Press [D] or the Aa button to look up or edit any pair’s word. The stats drawer shows your 10 slowest single pairs from the last 48 hours, with their words. The clock at the top left is your session time.

Multi-pair attempts

When Pairs per attempt is 2 or more, the final time keeps your accent colour and the attempt is left out of the heatmap and the fast/medium/slow counts, since those thresholds are for single pairs.
